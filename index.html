— FAQS —

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our vehicle shipping, customs clearances, or D2D logistics? Here are the most common queries answered.

Simply share your parts requirements with Autohub. Our team coordinates with the relevant suppliers to source the required parts, manages pricing and procurement, and arranges logistics. We provide visibility into your order status, helping you stay informed from initial enquiry through to final delivery.

AUTOHUB is a dedicated logistics, shipping, and supply-chain provider rather than a motor vehicle dealership. However, through our Dealer Intelligence Platform (DIP) and long-established network of accredited exporters in Japan, the UK, Australia, and the USA, we assist registered dealerships and private buyers with vehicle sourcing verification, inspection, and door-to-door transport.

Our Door-to-Door (D2D) package is an all-inclusive fixed-price logistics service. We collect the vehicle from auction yards or dealerships in the country of origin, conduct pre-shipment inspections, handle export clearance and sea freight, manage destination customs and MPI biosecurity clearance, and transport the car directly to your compliance workshop or designated address with guaranteed zero hidden surcharges.

Most vehicles are transported inside state-of-the-art Roll-On/Roll-Off (RORO) pure car carrier vessels, safely sheltered inside enclosed multi-level decks from marine weather elements. For high-value classics, race cars, or non-running project vehicles, we also offer secure 20ft and 40ft dedicated ocean containers with custom wheel chocking and lashing.

Roll-On/Roll-Off (RORO) refers to specialized ocean vessels with heavy-duty ramp doors that allow wheeled cargo to be driven directly on and off the ship under controlled conditions. It eliminates the need for crane lifting, significantly minimizes handling risks, and represents the most cost-effective and dependable method of international car shipping worldwide.
